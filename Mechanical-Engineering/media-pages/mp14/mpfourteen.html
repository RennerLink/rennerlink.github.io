Stair Climber V2

This robot can climb stairs of various heights and depths, and navigate curving stairs as well. It features 24-wheel drive and uses tank steering for turning. (Can turn in place) 12 AA batteries power the six legs. The chassis makes very efficient use of motors, using three motors for raising the legs and two motors for steering/driving.
