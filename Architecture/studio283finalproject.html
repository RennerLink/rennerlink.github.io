Gate Garden Path Pavilion

Overview

I worked with Jake Green to create pavilions embedded into an artificial landscape that each display a work of art. Jake's pavilion features Women with Parasol, a painting by Claude Monet. My pavilion houses the sculpture Stairway by Nathan Sawaya. Both works of art display personal growth and stewardship.

Partner: Jake Green

Born in Juneau, Alaska, and raised in Coos Bay, Oregon. Partook in a high school foreign exchange program in Italy where he found his passion for Architecture, and is now a student at the University of Oregon's College of Design.

Project Timeline

2/17/2025 - 3/24/2025 (3 weeks)

Notes on initial constraints, abstract ideas for landscape, and figure ground.

Figure ground iterations involving one art to be positioned higher on a slope than the other representing the present themes of ascension and fostering.

Armature iterations evolved into new ideas of conveying heiarchy between the two art pieces through more extreme differences in elevation.

Concepts for finner details and user experience with stairs.

Initial ideas on new floorplan for upper pavilion and wall surface details.

Progression of ceiling height and changing floor elevation throughout pavilion.

Section cut of pavilion concept. Chaneling daylight to spotlight the Stairway sculpture. Storage for coats and bags carved into hallway wall.

Updated section cuts with re-orientation of hallway and gallery room.

A section cut drawing from a design one was misinterpreted into this new form. We challenged ourselves to use this new form in our existing typography.

Plan drawings with new form and initial floor plan arrangement.

One water pool was cut short to make room for a new stairway connecting the two halves of the model making each pavilion accessable from anywhere on the site.
