Design Skills 202

Instructor: Daisy-O’lice I. Williams

Daisy-O’lice I. Williams holds a Master of Architecture (2005) and a B.S. in Architectural Studies (2002) from Florida A&M University. She is an Associate Professor in the Department of Architecture, specializing in architectural visualization, media, and beginning design studios. Her teaching and research explore how design tools influence design thinking, with recent work focusing on digital and augmented reality collaging. Williams is also dedicated to diversifying architectural education and has published on race, gender, and HBCU history in architecture. She contributed to Freedom’s Fortress, a digital humanities project, and co-developed the PRW Career Mapper, a tool for exploring architect Paul R. Williams’s career. She previously taught at Hampton University, earning a Provost Innovative Teaching Award.
