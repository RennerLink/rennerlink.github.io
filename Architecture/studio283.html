Design Studio 283

Final Project

Stacked pavilions dislay two works of art in respectively different ways through embracing daylight among other design principles.

Hand Drawing

Seven Points in Collaboration

Working within a prescribed set of rules, you will generate a series of studies to begin a studio conversation about object relationships, your facility as a designer, and the value of the iterative process. These compositions are strictly self-referential- think of them solely regarding figure and ground. This is a collaborative project and will be done in teams of two.



Line Composition

Compose 12 line compositions, from which select the strongest four. For each of the top compositions draft with graphite and construct on chipboard.

Plane

Create four iterations by adding dimention to the previous line compositions. Select one composition to draw in elevation and diagram.

Mass (Inhabited Wall)

- To design thresholds, passages, alcoves, and small rooms in a thick wall.
- To dramatize the admission of daylight into three-dimensional space.
- To discover the potential of subtractive design.

Plaster Casting

Landform

Landform addresses the reality that nature and our building sites are rarely flat. Ground may also be manipulated, moved or removed to create engaging contexts, level changes, and new sight lines.

Canopy

As our final exploration of this sequence of work, you will add an overhead plane(s) to your design in the pursuit of meaningful, practical and poetic place-making. You might consider aspects of prospect and refuge, relief from the sun, dramatization of sunlight and implying new in-between sheltered edges to your design.
